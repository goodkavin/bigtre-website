# Big Tre

> Big Tre (บิ๊กเทร) is a Bangkok company for e-commerce brands that sell on Shopee, Lazada, TikTok Shop, Shopify, LINE and their own stores. It connects those channels read-only, turns them into one set of sales, stock and profit numbers that reconcile, and gives the team an AI data analyst that answers in Thai from those numbers.

Registered Thai company: บริษัท เงินมากมาย รวยไม่ จำกัด, trading as Big Tre, registration no. 0105568204216. Founders: Kavin Sermsaksakon (CTO) and Napat Rituyanont (COO).

Who it is for: Thai e-commerce brands with 20 to 500 million baht a year in sales, three or more sales channels and little or no in-house tech team. Fashion, beauty, jewellery and consumer goods.

What it does:

- **Data foundation**: orders, settlements and stock from every channel in one place, under written rules, reconciled to the platforms and the bank. Dashboards and scheduled reports on top.
- **AI data analyst**: a chat assistant in the team's work chat that answers questions in Thai from the company's own numbers and shows the rule behind each answer.
- **Free AI Readiness Health Check**: 10 questions online, a readiness band and a one-page roadmap by email, then a call with a founder.

Price: setup from about 60,000 baht, then from about 14,000 baht a month. The Health Check is free.

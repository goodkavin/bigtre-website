# Big Tre pricing

Big Tre charges a one-time setup fee and a monthly fee. Setup starts from {{price_setup_from_en}}. The monthly fee starts from {{price_monthly_from_en}}. The first step, the [AI Readiness Health Check](/en/ai-readiness-health-check), is free. The exact price is set in a written quotation after the Health Check call and about a week of learning the business.

## What each fee covers

| Fee | Covers |
|---|---|
| Setup, from {{price_setup_from_en}} | Connecting the brand's systems, writing and confirming the business rules, reconciling the first month, dashboards and reports, setting up the [AI data analyst](/en/ai-data-analyst) |
| Monthly, from {{price_monthly_from_en}} | Daily data refresh, keeping rules and product mappings current as SKUs and channels change, the AI data analyst, scheduled reports and alerts, fixes |

## What moves the price

- The number of systems and channels to connect.
- How many product codes need mapping, and how many sets and bundles there are.
- Whether cost of goods, settlements and ads are in scope or only sales and stock.
- How many reports need to be produced in the team's own layout.

## Common questions

### Is VAT added?

Big Tre is not registered for VAT at the time of writing, so no VAT is added. For a VAT-registered buyer this also means there is no input VAT to claim. The quotation states the terms.

### Is there a minimum term?

The term is set in each quotation. Current contracts start with a short pilot that either side can stop by written notice before the next service month, then move to a longer agreement. See [data ownership and exit](/en/data-ownership-security).

### Why not a fixed software subscription?

Most of the work is fitting the numbers to how each brand runs: its rules, codes and reports. A packaged dashboard skips that step, which is why its numbers often disagree with the accounts.

### What does the Health Check cost?

Nothing. It is 10 questions online, an emailed one-page roadmap and a call with a founder.

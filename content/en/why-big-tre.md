# Why e-commerce brands choose Big Tre

Thai e-commerce brands choose Big Tre when they want sales, stock and profit numbers they can trust across every channel, and an AI that answers from those numbers, without hiring a data team. It works on the systems the brand already runs, writes the brand's rules down, and charges a setup fee and a monthly fee.

## The reasons, with evidence

| Reason | Evidence |
|---|---|
| It works inside Thai e-commerce | Its clients sell on Shopee, Lazada, TikTok Shop, Shopify, LINE and in stores, on Zort and Shipnity. Marketplace fees, platform and seller vouchers, free gifts and sets are handled as standard |
| Rules first, then dashboards | Every client gets a written rulebook confirmed on its own orders before any dashboard. Each number traces to a rule |
| It is honest about what it cannot compute | Profit after ad spend is shown as blocked until ad accounts are connected, not estimated. The [integrations](/en/integrations) page marks what runs today and what is built on request |
| The AI answers in Thai from the brand's own data | The [AI data analyst](/en/ai-data-analyst) runs in the team's Telegram chat for both current clients |
| Read-only, separate per client | Big Tre does not write to client systems. Each client's data and AI analyst are kept apart |
| It keeps running after setup | A monthly fee covers the daily refresh, new SKUs and channels, reports and the AI analyst |
| Starting prices stated up front | Setup from {{price_setup_from_en}}, then from {{price_monthly_from_en}}. See [pricing](/en/pricing) |
| The founders have built these systems before | The CTO spent eight years on ERP, data and AI systems for Thai companies and co-founded MineERP. The CEO leads client work, from process definition to weekly follow-up |

## Who it suits best

- A brand on three or more channels whose reports do not agree.
- A brand that wants margin per SKU and per channel after fees and vouchers.
- A brand that tried AI and got answers it could not trust.
- An owner who is the only person able to answer questions about the business.

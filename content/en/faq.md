# Big Tre FAQ

Short answers about Big Tre, a company in {{city_en}} that builds trusted sales, stock and profit data and AI data analysts for Thai e-commerce brands.

## The company

### What does Big Tre do?

It connects a brand's sales channels and systems read-only, writes down the brand's rules for sales, costs and stock, and builds one set of numbers that reconcile. On top it sets up dashboards, scheduled reports and an [AI data analyst](/en/ai-data-analyst) that answers questions in Thai.

### Who is Big Tre for?

Thai e-commerce brands with roughly 20 to 500 million baht in yearly sales, three or more sales channels and little or no tech team. Its clients are in beauty and jewellery.

### Does Big Tre work with SMEs?

Yes. Most fit the SME range: growing brands with roughly 20 to 500 million baht a year in sales. Selling on several channels with numbers that disagree matters more than size.

### How does it start?

With the free [AI Readiness Health Check](/en/ai-readiness-health-check): 10 questions online, a roadmap by email, and a call with a founder within 48 hours. If there is a fit, Big Tre spends about a week learning the business and sends a written plan and quotation.

### How long until the numbers are live?

Rules, the reconciled foundation and dashboards in the first 6 weeks. Automated reports and alerts in months 2 to 4.

### Do we need a tech team?

No. Big Tre does the technical work. The team's part is answering questions about how the business runs and confirming the rules.

## Systems and data

### Which systems does Big Tre connect?

Running for clients: Zort, Shipnity, Shopee, Lazada and TikTok Shop settlement reports, Google Sheets and store POS. On request: BigSeller, Shopify, ERPs, accounting software, ad accounts and carriers. See [integrations](/en/integrations).

### Do we have to replace our order management system or ERP?

No. Big Tre reads from the systems in use and does not replace them.

### Does Big Tre change anything in our systems?

No. Connections are read-only.

### Where is our data stored?

In Google Cloud (BigQuery), Singapore region, separate for each client. The client owns its data, reports and rules, and receives them within 30 days if the contract ends. See [data ownership and exit](/en/data-ownership-security).

### Which AI does the data analyst use?

Anthropic's Claude. It reads only that client's data and the client's written rules.

### Does Big Tre do our accounting or tax?

No. It makes the management numbers correct and gives the accountant clean files. Filing stays with the accountant.

# Free AI Readiness Health Check

The AI Readiness Health Check is Big Tre's free first step for e-commerce brands and retailers that want to use AI. It is a 10-question online form that takes about 3 minutes. It places the business in one of three readiness bands, emails a one-page roadmap, and a founder follows up within 48 hours. [Start the Health Check](/ai-readiness/).

## Where to start with AI, in three steps

1. **Put the numbers in one place, under one definition.** Sales, stock and margin from every channel, reconciled. AI on top of mismatched exports gives confident wrong answers.
2. **Automate the recurring reports.** The weekly and monthly reports the team rebuilds by hand.
3. **Add AI that answers from those numbers.** Questions in Thai, in the team's chat, with the source of each figure.

The Health Check shows which step a business is on.

## What it asks

| Area | Example question |
|---|---|
| Operations | Can you answer "how did we do last month?" in under 5 minutes? |
| Operations | How much of how the business runs is only in the owner's head? |
| Visibility | Can you see profit per channel after fees, discounts and shipping? |
| Visibility | After a campaign, do you know what it cost in margin, not only spend? |
| Trust in numbers | Does reported revenue match bank deposits each month? |
| Data sources | How many places do you pull numbers from? |
| AI | Has AI been tried in the business, and did it stick? |
| About the business | Type of business, yearly sales and number of sales channels |

## The three bands

| Band | What it means |
|---|---|
| Foundation | The numbers are not yet in one place or not trusted. AI on top would give unreliable answers. Start with the [data foundation](/en/data-foundation) |
| Building | Some numbers are trusted and some are not. Fix the gaps the check names, then add AI on the parts that are ready |
| AI-Ready | The numbers are in one place and trusted. An [AI data analyst](/en/ai-data-analyst) and automated reports can start now |

## What happens after

1. The band and the three biggest gaps are shown on screen.
2. A one-page roadmap arrives by email within minutes: what to build, in what order, on what timeline.
3. A Big Tre founder contacts the business within 48 hours to talk it through.
4. If there is a fit, Big Tre spends about a week learning the business and gives a written plan and quotation. No commitment is required at any step.

## A self-check before starting

If two of these are true, the business is in the Foundation band and AI tools will struggle:

- Two reports of last month's sales give different totals.
- Profit per channel after fees takes days to work out.
- Revenue and bank deposits are not reconciled each month.
- The same product has a different code on each marketplace.

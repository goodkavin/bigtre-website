# Systems Big Tre connects

Big Tre connects read-only to the systems a Thai e-commerce brand already runs and brings their data into one [data foundation](/en/data-foundation). It does not replace them. The table says which connections are running for a client today and which are built when a client needs them.

| System | Examples | What it gives | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| Order management system | Zort, Shipnity | Orders and items from every channel, daily, stock and days of cover | Running for clients |
| Order management system | BigSeller and others with an API or export | The same | Built on request |
| Marketplace settlement and fee reports | Shopee, Lazada, TikTok Shop | Payouts with every fee itemised, seller-paid and platform-paid discounts split | Running for clients |
| TikTok affiliate reports | TikTok Shop affiliate | Affiliate and creator sales | Running for clients |
| Google Sheets and Drive | Cost sheets, targets, campaign calendars | Hand-kept inputs joined to the live data; reports written back in the team's own layout | Running for clients |
| Store POS | Retail branches | Store sales next to online sales | Running for clients |
| Own web store | Shopify, WooCommerce, LINE Shopping | Web-store margin, repeat customers, discount codes | Built on request |
| Warehouse or 3PL | In-house WMS, fulfilment providers | Counted stock against system stock, defects and returns split | Built on request |
| ERP | Odoo, ERPNext, SAP Business One | Purchase cost into true cost of goods and margin per SKU | Built on request |
| Accounting | Express, PEAK, FlowAccount | Management figures next to the books, month-end files for the accountant | Built on request |
| Ads | Meta, Google, TikTok Ads | Profit after ad spend, return on ad spend by campaign | Built on request |

"Built on request" means Big Tre has the design and connects it as part of a project. It is not a ready-made plug-in.

## Using Zort or Shipnity?

Zort and Shipnity already collect orders from every channel and show sales and stock reports. Big Tre reads from them through their APIs or exports and adds what they do not hold: settlement fees and payouts per order, cost of goods, margin per SKU and channel, the brand's own definitions, reports in the team's Google Sheets layout, and the [AI data analyst](/en/ai-data-analyst) that answers questions in Thai. Both are running for current clients.

## How a connection is made

- Through the system's API where it has one, with a key the client issues and can revoke.
- Through scheduled exports or files in a shared Drive folder where it has none.
- Read-only. Big Tre does not change orders, stock, prices or accounts.

## Common questions

**Do we have to change our order management system?** No. Big Tre reads from the one in use.

**Our product codes differ between Shopee, Lazada and our OMS.** This is the most common problem. Big Tre keeps a mapping of every channel's code to one product, and updates it as new SKUs appear.

**We still keep some numbers in spreadsheets.** That is fine. Costs, targets and campaign dates often live in Google Sheets and are read from there.

# Big Tre work

Big Tre has built data foundations and AI data analysts for Thai e-commerce brands in beauty and jewellery. Both clients below are paying and running in production. Client names are withheld.

| Client | Channels and systems | What was built | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| Multi-brand beauty seller | Shopee, Lazada, TikTok Shop; Zort | Daily data pipeline, margin model, automated reports per brand, monthly company P&L, AI data analyst | Live, monthly subscription |
| Jewellery brand | Shopee, TikTok Shop, Lazada, Shopify, LINE and about 10 retail stores; Shipnity | Daily data pipeline, dashboards, reports in Google Sheets, AI data analyst, a Shopify app | Live, monthly subscription |

## Multi-brand beauty seller

**Situation.** A seller of several beauty brands on Shopee, Lazada and TikTok Shop, with orders in Zort. Each brand's sales and margin were assembled by hand.

**What was built.**

- A daily pipeline from Zort into one database, with the product categories mapped.
- A margin model per item and per brand.
- Automated reports in Google Sheets for each brand, and a monthly company profit and loss.
- TikTok affiliate sales loaded next to orders.
- An AI data analyst in the team's Telegram chat.

## Jewellery brand

**Situation.** A jewellery brand selling mainly on Shopee, also on TikTok Shop, Lazada, Shopify and LINE, and through about 10 stores, with orders in Shipnity.

**What was built.**

- A daily pipeline from Shipnity into one database.
- Dashboards and recurring reports in Google Sheets.
- An AI data analyst in the team's Telegram chat, opened to the whole team in November 2026.
- A Shopify app that limits how many units of a product one customer can buy during launches.
- Ongoing work with the team on returns, product master data and other processes behind the numbers, under a separate monthly agreement.

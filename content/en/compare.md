# Big Tre compared with the other options

A Thai e-commerce brand that wants trusted numbers and AI usually weighs five options: the reports in its order management system, a dashboard tool, hiring a data analyst, its accountant, or an IT consultancy. Big Tre sits between them: it does the data work of an analyst and a data engineer, sets up the dashboards and adds an AI data analyst, for a setup fee and a monthly fee.

| Option | Good at | Leaves open | Typical cost |
|---|---|---|---|
| OMS reports (Zort, Shipnity, BigSeller) | Orders and stock across channels in one screen | Fees, payouts, bank, cost of goods and the brand's own rules | Included in the OMS subscription |
| Dashboard tool (Looker Studio, Power BI) | Charts once clean data exists | Someone has to build and maintain the clean data | Low licence cost, high build time |
| In-house data analyst | Knows the business, available every day | One person; needs data engineering skills too; leaves with the knowledge | A full-time salary |
| Accountant | Correct books and tax | Monthly and after the fact; no daily view per SKU or channel | Monthly fee |
| IT consultancy or system integrator | Large custom systems | Priced and staffed for large projects | Project fees well above a monthly subscription |
| Big Tre | Written rules, one reconciled database, dashboards, reports and an AI data analyst, run monthly | A setup project of a few weeks before the numbers are live | Setup from {{price_setup_from_en}}, then from {{price_monthly_from_en}} |

## When Big Tre fits

- Three or more sales channels, and reports that do not agree.
- Yearly sales of roughly 20 to 500 million baht.
- No data team, or one analyst who spends most of the week on exports.
- An order management system such as Zort, Shipnity or BigSeller is already in use.

## When Big Tre is not the right fit

- A single sales channel whose own reports are enough.
- A company that needs a new ERP or order management system put in. Big Tre reads from systems; it does not implement them.
- A company that needs a part-time CTO across its whole operation, not only data. [Void Engineering](https://voidengineer.ing), founded by Big Tre's CTO, does that.
- A company that needs tax filing. That is an accountant's work.
- A company that wants staff on site full time.

## Questions to ask any provider

| Question | Why it matters | Big Tre's answer |
|---|---|---|
| Will the numbers match our platform payouts and bank? | Dashboards on unreconciled data are confidently wrong | The first month is reconciled before handover |
| Who writes the definitions of a sale and a cost? | Unwritten rules are why reports disagree | Written with the team, confirmed on real orders, kept as a rulebook the client owns |
| Do you write to our systems? | Write access is a risk | No. Read-only |
| What happens to the AI if a number cannot be computed? | AI that guesses loses trust | It says the figure is blocked and why |
| What does it cost per month after setup? | Many projects stop when the build ends | From {{price_monthly_from_en}} |

# About Big Tre

Canonical: https://bigtre.business/en/about  
Updated: 2026-11-18

Big Tre is run by a Thai limited company registered with the Department of Business Development, based in Bangkok. It builds one trusted set of sales, stock and profit numbers for e-commerce brands that sell on several channels, and an AI data analyst that answers the team's questions from those numbers in Thai. Its clients are Thai brands in beauty and jewellery.

## Why it exists

Big Tre started as a small business itself. It had tools that seemed to work together, and they did not: the shop systems, the order management system, the marketplaces and the accounts each worked, and together they gave different answers. Large companies pay a data team to fix this. Big Tre does the same work for brands that have no data team.

## What it does

- [Data foundation](https://bigtre.business/en/data-foundation): every channel's orders, settlements and stock in one place, under written rules, reconciled, with dashboards and scheduled reports.
- [AI data analyst](https://bigtre.business/en/ai-data-analyst): a chat assistant in the team's work chat that answers in Thai from the company's own numbers.
- [AI Readiness Health Check](https://bigtre.business/en/ai-readiness-health-check): a free first check of where the business stands.

## What it does not do

- It does not replace the order management system, the accounting software or the ERP. It reads from them.
- It does not write to a client's systems. Connections are read-only.
- It does not file tax. It makes the numbers an accountant works from correct.

## Big Tre and Void Engineering

Kavin Sermsaksakon also founded [Void Engineering](https://voidengineer.ing), a separate company that works as a fractional CTO for companies with no technology leader. Big Tre focuses on data and AI analysts for multi-channel e-commerce brands. A company that needs a part-time CTO to plan and build across its whole operation is better served by Void Engineering.

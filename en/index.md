# Big Tre: one set of numbers and an AI data analyst for Thai e-commerce brands

Canonical: https://bigtre.business/en  
Updated: 2026-11-18

Big Tre is a company in Bangkok that works with e-commerce brands selling on Shopee, Lazada, TikTok Shop, Shopify, LINE and their own stores. It connects every channel read-only, writes down the rules for what counts as a sale, a cost and a unit of stock, and builds one set of sales, stock and profit numbers that reconcile with the platforms and the bank. On top of those numbers it gives the team dashboards, scheduled reports and an AI data analyst that answers questions in Thai.

## Problems it is built for

- Sales from Shopee, Lazada, TikTok Shop and Shopify never match each other or the bank. See [why marketplace numbers disagree](https://bigtre.business/en/marketplace-numbers-dont-match).
- Nobody can say which SKU makes money on which channel after fees, vouchers and returns. See [real profit per SKU](https://bigtre.business/en/profit-per-sku).
- Weekly reports are rebuilt by hand from exports every week.
- The owner is the only person who can answer questions about the business.
- AI was tried and gave answers nobody could trust, because the data under it was not organised.

## How the work runs

1. **Health Check.** A short online check and a call with a founder.
2. **Rules.** Big Tre interviews the people who know how the business runs and writes the rules down: which orders count, how fees work, how sets and returns move stock. Each rule is confirmed with the client on its own data.
3. **Foundation.** Every channel flows into one database every day, under those rules, and is reconciled.
4. **Use.** Dashboards, reports in the Google Sheets the team already uses, and the AI data analyst in the team chat.

Typical timing: rules, foundation and dashboards in the first 6 weeks; automated reports and alerts in months 2 to 4.

Updated 2026-11-18

"Built on request" means Big Tre has the design and connects it as part of a project. It is not a ready-made plug-in.

Using Zort or Shipnity?

Zort and Shipnity already collect orders from every channel and show sales and stock reports. Big Tre reads from them through their APIs or exports and adds what they do not hold: settlement fees and payouts per order, cost of goods, margin per SKU and channel, the brand's own definitions, reports in the team's Google Sheets layout, and the AI data analyst that answers questions in Thai. Both are running for current clients.

How a connection is made

  • Through the system's API where it has one, with a key the client issues and can revoke.
  • Through scheduled exports or files in a shared Drive folder where it has none.
  • Read-only. Big Tre does not change orders, stock, prices or accounts.

Common questions

Do we have to change our order management system? No. Big Tre reads from the one in use.

Our product codes differ between Shopee, Lazada and our OMS. This is the most common problem. Big Tre keeps a mapping of every channel's code to one product, and updates it as new SKUs appear.

We still keep some numbers in spreadsheets. That is fine. Costs, targets and campaign dates often live in Google Sheets and are read from there.
